Reading list

Books for student founders

The books I’d hand a student who wants to build something, from the appendix of The Student Founder.

By Andrew Ashur, founder and CEO of Lucid Bots · November 19, 2026

These are the eleven books on the reading list at the back of The Student Founder. Your library probably has most of them, and used copies cost a few dollars.

Strategy and mindset

  • Zero to One by Peter Thiel. On finding the secrets in markets other people overlook.
  • Shoe Dog by Phil Knight. The most honest account I know of how fragile a great company feels from the inside.
  • The Right It by Alberto Savoia. How to test whether an idea is worth building before you spend a dollar.
  • Failure Is An Option by Mike Grossman. A founder’s book that gives the failures as much room as the wins, and is funny about them.

Execution and focus

  • Deep Work by Cal Newport. The case for protecting your focus hours.
  • Atomic Habits by James Clear. How small daily behaviors compound.
  • Build by Tony Fadell. From the person who built the iPod and Nest. Especially useful for hardware founders.

Sales and customers

  • The Mom Test by Rob Fitzpatrick. How to run customer conversations that get honest answers.
  • Influence by Robert Cialdini. Why people say yes.
  • Founding Sales by Pete Kazanjy. How to win your first paying customers without a sales team.

Fundraising

  • Venture Deals by Brad Feld and Jason Mendelson. Term sheets, SAFEs and dilution. Read it before your first investor meeting.
